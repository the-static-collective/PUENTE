INTERCEPTED TRANSMISSION

What are the rules?

These are conditional instructions, not hints about buttons. Read every statement and deduce a configuration that satisfies all of them.

    Select three relays that create a path. Then test your theory.

    Operator reference

    Si... entonces = if... then. Solo uno = exactly one. Por lo menos uno = at least one. Mismo estado = same state.

    You are balancing two systems: a path through the physical circuit and a unique combination of logical constraints. The game verifies both.
